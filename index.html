We Believe

Together we can deliver health promotion programs that we know improve health outcomes. We can create spaces and opportunities to take action over our health, advocate for our needs and make positive change in our community.

Community-Based Participatory Research

We collaborate with our members to make sure their voices and lived experiences are included in programs, research, and partnerships.

Evidence-Based Public Health Programming

Our health promotion programs are based on current literature, theory, and best practices that are shown to improve health outcomes.

Culturally Tailored Education and Resources

We provide accessible, culturally relevant education and resouces that reflect the needs of Latino communities.

Community Building and Capacity

We create bonds, strengthen relationships, empower our people and build long-term community capacity
