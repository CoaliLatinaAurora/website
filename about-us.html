Our Story

How We Began

Coalición Latina para un Aurora Saludable was established in 2026 with a simple but powerful belief: healthier communities are built when people have the knowledge, resources, and opportunities to shape their own well-being.

Our story began with a recognition that Latino communities continue to face barriers to accessing culturally relevant health information, education, and opportunities to participate in decisions that affect their health.

We founded Coalición Latina to build upon the strengths of our communities and create a space where community voices guide meaningful action.

Based in Aurora, Colorado, our organization brings together public health research, evidence-based health promotion, and culturally responsive education to support healthier Latino communities.
