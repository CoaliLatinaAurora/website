Current Research Initiatives

Acculturative Stress & Latino Health

This research investigates the drivers of acculturative stress in Latino communities. It is the stress that is a result of adapting to a new environment, culture and way of life. Aculturative stress manifests differently in physical and mental health outcomes.

Through qualitative research, we are conducting interviews to help us better understand the lived experiences of Latinos in Aurora who had to learn to navigate new systems, establish support networks, and maintain their cultural identity.

Our hope is to use the findings to improve awareness of acculturative stress and it's impact on health, increase advocacy and resources to help mititage it early on and inform public health programming. You can help us by volunteering to do a 30-45 minute interview in-person or over the phone. Contact us to find out more.
