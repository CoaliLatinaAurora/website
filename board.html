Board of Directors

Our Board of Directors provides leadership, guidance, and oversight to advance our mission of improving the health and well-being of Latino communities. Through their diverse experience and shared committment to community empowerment, our directors help shape the future of the Latino Coalition for a Healthy Aurora.

Ivonne Parra MPH

President

Ivonne has over 10 years of experience in public health research She has been committed to her community since her birth.

Omar Coronado Garcia RN BSN

Vice President

Omar has spent his career as a licensed nurse, working in hospital systems and now working closely with our community in a home-health setting. Omar has a passion to educate and inform our community on health related topics like diabetes, blood pressure management, & health literacy.

Diana Flores MA

Treasurer

Diana has worked as a project manager for the Colorado Department of Public Health's environmental monitoring unit. She has worked closely with the Latino community in many areas.

Heisel

Secretary

Heisel works in APD, has been a math tutor for our community and uses her skills to help us advance.

Shane Esmundo DrPH MPH

Member at Large

Shane is an expert in public health research, she is an advocate for many and has been involved in community projects for years.
