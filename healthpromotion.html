Health Promotion

Promoting healthier lifestyles and stronger communities.

Our Approach

We believe everyone deserves the opportunity to live a healthy and active life.

Our health promotion efforts focus on reducing barriers, encouraging healthy behaviors, and creating culturally relevant opportunities that support the well-being of Latino communities.

Physical Activity

Physical activity plays an important role in improving physical, mental, and social well-being.

We seek to create accessible opportunities for community members to participate in walking, running, hiking, cycling, and other recreational activities.

Through community-centered initiatives, we aim to encourage active lifestyles, strengthen social connections, and make movement an enjoyable part of everyday life.

Get Involved

Help us build a healthier, more active Aurora. Whether you're interested in participating in our activities, volunteering, or partnering with us, we welcome your involvement.
