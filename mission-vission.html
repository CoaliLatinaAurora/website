Our Mission

To advance Latino community health in Aurora through culturally tailored public health initiatives that strengthen community capacity, promote health equity, and create opportunities for advocacy and community-based research.

Our Vision

Our vision is a thriving, equitable Aurora where Latino communities across generations have the knowledge, connections, and opportunities to achieve lifelong health and well-being.

Our Guiding Principles

The principles that guide our work and commitment to the community.

Community

Centering community voices and experiences

Collaboration

Building partnerships that inspire action

Education

Making health knowledge accessible

Equity

Addressing barriers to health and opportunity

Research

Using evidence to amplify our voices and inform community solutions.

Empowerment

Supporting communities in shaping their future
